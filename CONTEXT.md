# RateMyBusiness

Job seekers rate the hiring process at a company, not the workplace, the job, or a named interviewer.

## Language

**Company**:
The employer whose hiring process is being rated.
_Avoid_: business, employer page, organization

**Candidate**:
A signed-in person who may write a Review.
_Avoid_: user, reviewer, account

**Review**:
One Candidate’s account of one hiring process at one Company.
_Avoid_: rating, feedback, post

**Score**:
An integer from 1 to 5 on a named dimension of the hiring process. A Review's overall is the mean of its five Scores, to one decimal.
_Avoid_: star, grade

**Stage reached**:
The furthest step the Candidate got to, in order: applied, screened, interviewed, final round, offered.
_Avoid_: status, step

**Ending**:
How the process ended for the Candidate: hired, declined (an offer), rejected, withdrew, or ghosted. Hired and declined only follow the offered stage.
_Avoid_: result, outcome status

**Outcome track**:
The drawing of Stage reached plus Ending on a Review: a line through the stages that ends in a mark for the Ending.

**Imported review**:
A Review summarized, in our own words, from an interview experience a candidate posted publicly. It links to its source and its Scores are estimated. Each belongs to a hidden Candidate that cannot sign in.
_Avoid_: fake review, scraped review

**Company dossier**:
The public Company page: identity, aggregate scores, where Candidates ended up, and the list of Reviews.
_Avoid_: profile, company page
